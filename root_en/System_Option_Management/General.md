**Password Policy Management: Formulate policy principles for PowerMoat system account passwords**

-   Minimum Length (characters): Set minimum password length.

-   At least 1 lowercase letter: Password must contain at least 1 lowercase letter.

-   At least 1 uppercase letter: Password must contain at least 1 uppercase letter.

-   至少1個number：密碼至少包含1個number。

-   At least 1 special character: Password must contain at least 1 special character.

-   Do not repeat with previous N passwords: Password cannot repeat with previous N.

-   Effective Days: Password must be changed after effective days.

**Account Lockout: Formulate policy for PowerMoat system account login failures**

-   Enable: Whether to enable

-   Login Failure Count: Account will be locked if failure count exceeds limit.

-   Lockout Time (minutes): After account lockout, lock will be automatically released after this many minutes.
