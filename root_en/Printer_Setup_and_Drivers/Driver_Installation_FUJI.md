  #@img_A0004/0006.png

  #@img_A0004/0007.png

  #@img_A0004/0008.png

- Continue with the installation, select the driver
  
  #@img_A0004/0009.png

  #@img_A0004/0010.png

- Complete the installation

  #@img_A0004/0011.png
